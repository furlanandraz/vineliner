[general]
name=Vineliner
qgisMinimumVersion=3.40
description=Finds vineyard rows in aerial or drone imagery and draws each row as a line.
about=Vineliner searches each vineyard polygon for parallel rows with a weighted five-line template over every rotation, row width and position, then grows, joins and checks the rows.
    Inputs: an RGB layer, a false-colour NIR-R-G layer and/or an NDVI layer (at least one; with NIR-R-G, NDVI is computed when no NDVI layer is given), the vineyard polygons, and the range of row widths to search.
    Outputs: the rows as lines with one attribute, needs_review, and the vineyard polygons with average row length, direction and width and a 95-percent confidence interval of the width.
    Requires NumPy, SciPy and Shapely 2 in QGIS's Python (included with QGIS 3.40 and later on Windows).
